# Status writing

- Lead with the outcome, then the detail. "Checkout now saves carts" beats "Worked on carts".
- One line per item. Link or name the work item.
- Group by result (Done, In progress, Blocked), not by person.
- Say what is blocked and what would unblock it.
- No filler. Cut "just", "basically" and "as per".
- Keep numbers honest. If you did not count it, do not give a count.
